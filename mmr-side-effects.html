3. When side effects show up

MMR is a live, weakened virus, so most reactions appear one to two weeks after the shot, when the vaccine virus is multiplying, not on the day. Days after vaccination.

Measles inclusion body encephalitis, which happens only in people with severely weakened immune systems, has appeared 4–9 months after vaccination (CDC). A dashed edge means the start or end is not sharply defined.

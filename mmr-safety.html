2. What the main studies found

Each line compares autism risk in children who got MMR with children who didn't. The square is the best estimate; the line is the 95% confidence interval (the range the true value very likely falls in). A value of 1 means no difference. Every interval includes 1, so none of these studies found a meaningful increase in risk, including in high-risk children.

7. What MMR can actually cause, next to what measles causes

The vaccine does have real, well-documented side effects. They are far rarer and milder than the harms of the disease. Log scale: each gridline is 10× the one before.

MMR vaccine, per million doses Measles infection, per million cases

Bars show a range where sources give one. Vaccine rates are per million doses (CDC); measles rates are per million cases (CDC; SSPE in young children from a California study: 1 in 609 when infected under age 1, 1 in 1,367 under age 5). A Cochrane review puts febrile seizures from MMR-family vaccines somewhat higher, at 1 in 1,150–1,700 doses. The two sets use different denominators, but without vaccination most children eventually catch measles. MMR never contained thimerosal, the mercury-based preservative some vaccine fears focus on (CDC).
